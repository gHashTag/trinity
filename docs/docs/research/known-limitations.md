**Disclosure, 2026-08-12 — the G and Ω rows above are one-parameter calibrations, and by this document's own criterion they belong on the other side of the table.**

π³γ²/φ = **1.067914**, γ⁸π⁴/φ² = **0.000359**, γ⁴π²/φ = **0.018944**. All three are dimensionless numbers. Each is multiplied by a scale factor fitted to the single measurement it is then compared against: `G_SCALE ≈ 6.25×10⁻¹¹` for G, `OMEGA_COARSE_SCALE ≈ 1908.84` for Ω_Λ, and an implied ≈13.7 for Ω_DM. `t27/docs/nona-02-organism/physics-kepler/KEPLER-NEWTON-VERIFICATION.md` states this plainly and calls it "empirical calibration to match measurements"; it also lists theoretical justification for those scale factors as unfinished work.

With one free multiplicative parameter fitted to one target, the residual is zero by construction. So "0.09% error", "CONFIRMED" and "SMOKING GUN" on these three rows do not carry the evidential weight those labels imply — a fit cannot miss.

This matters most here, because this page's whole argument is that the α, μ and θ_W entries fail on **post-hoc fitting rather than theoretical necessity**. G and the Ω parameters fail the identical test: their scale factors were chosen after seeing CODATA and Planck. Judged by the standard this document applies three sections above, they belong in the NUMEROLOGY WARNING column until `G_SCALE` and `OMEGA_COARSE_SCALE` are derived rather than fitted. Two caveats sharpen the point rather than soften it: the α, μ and θ_W attempts at least produce dimensionless targets that a dimensionless formula could in principle hit, whereas G is compared against a dimensional quantity that φ-mathematics cannot produce at all; and the *rows have been left exactly as written*, because the record of the misclassification is the thing this page is for.
